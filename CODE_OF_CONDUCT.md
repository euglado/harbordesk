# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want HarborDesk to be welcoming and respectful for contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and respectful.
- Discuss ideas and code rather than personal traits.
- Accept clear, actionable feedback and respect differing viewpoints.
- Keep shared project spaces free from harassment, insults, and unwanted attention.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized attention without consent, and persistent disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately using GitHub's contact options for the repository owner. Reports will be reviewed fairly and handled as confidentially as possible. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to protect the community.
